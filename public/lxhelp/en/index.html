Getting started

Choose Ancient Remains Atlas, Documents Atlas, LXDNA Tree or LXDNA Control from the LXHAZZAA portal. “Coming soon” means a service is still in preparation; a portal card does not mean every tool is publicly available.

This is the shared help centre. Choose a guide from the navigation, or use Help in the relevant platform’s header.

Account and access

Public content can be browsed without an account. Use Sign in / Register for account services. Contribution and administration require an approved role; signing in does not grant reference-management or review access.

If a restricted tool returns you to a coming-soon page, check your session and role. Never send a password or sign-in code to support.

Language and appearance

Use the language selector where available and the day/night control to change appearance. In LX Help, switch guide language using the header link.

Privacy and policies

Read the terms and privacy policy. Sharing data with a testing service does not automatically authorise publication here. Record, original-file and personal-data access depend on the platform and permissions.

Support and corrections

Email lxhazzaa@gmail.com with the platform, record or kit ID and steps to reproduce the issue. Redact personal details in screenshots. Scientific corrections should include the original study or evidence and the disputed field.

Do not email private genetic files or sensitive documents without arranging an appropriate method first.
