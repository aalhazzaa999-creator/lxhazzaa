Usage, sources and limitations, within LXHAZZAA.

Availability and collections

The public landing page currently says Coming soon. Work and contribution pages require authorisation. This guide does not unlock access or announce a public catalogue launch.

The scope includes documents, records, deeds, letters, contracts, manuscripts, inscriptions and supporting references. When available, search covers published and reviewed materials, not private uploads by default.

Submitting a material

  1. Enter material type, title, original language and writing system.
  2. Record custodian, shelfmark, owner, provider and researcher in their respective roles, with source URL and citation.
  3. State access rights and supporting evidence. Without image permission, choose metadata only and do not upload the original.
  4. When authorised, attach PDF/JPG/PNG up to 15MB, confirm your declaration and submit for review.

Saving and submitting do not mean publication. Follow contribution status in your account when the tool is enabled.

Transcription and translation

Record the original-language transcription first, then its translation. Distinguish partial readings and drafts from reviewed text. Mark damaged or unreadable portions rather than presenting conjecture as fact.

Interface language does not change the source language. Translation remains a draft until reviewed; a document image alone does not verify every reading.

Rights, display and download

Permission to describe a material is not permission to publish or download its image. Availability may be metadata only, view only, or view and download, subject to rights and review. Attribution alone does not replace permission.

For Ottoman archive entries, use the official Turkish State Archives catalogue and shelfmark. This is a source citation, not a partnership or automatic image import.

Review and corrections

An authorised reviewer checks provenance, rights, description and text status before availability. For corrections or rights concerns, email support with the record ID, disputed field or right and supporting evidence. Do not attach a confidential original to a public report.
